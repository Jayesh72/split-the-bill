# Split The Bill

## Product

Split The Bill lets users scan a restaurant bill,
assign items to people, calculate exact shares,
and share the final result.

## MVP

The MVP does not require authentication.

## Main flow

1. Landing page
2. Start splitting
3. Upload / camera / manual bill
4. OCR extraction
5. Review extracted items
6. Add people
7. Assign items
8. Split shared items
9. Calculate taxes and charges
10. Review final split
11. Share result
